5 Things I Love About Fishing

Introduction

Stress and depression have become a serious problem in this generation. So instead of just being isolated in your room with nothing but walls and a depressing vibe, why not go out and embrace mother nature, pick up a rod and a line, set it up with a hook and go fishing? Fishing is more than just catching a fish. It is an experience that allows us to relax, explore nature and teaches us valuable skills. People may see it as just a hobby, but to me, it's one of the reasons that keeps me going in life. My goal here is to show you why I love fishing.

5 things I love about fishing

1. It helps you relax

Some might think that people who love to go fishing just go to catch fish, but no, fishing gives you an opportunity to take a break from your busy daily routine. Being surrounded by water, trees, and fresh air can create a peaceful environment and help you unwind.

2. Fishes can't hear you talk above the surface

When you talk above the water, most of your voice's sound travels through the air and only some of it enters the water. Fish can detect underwater vibrations and sounds, but they don't hear voices exactly like humans do. Because of that, I can play my favorite music while fishing.

3. You never know what you'll catch

One of the most exciting things about fishing is the element of surprise. You might spend hours waiting for a bite, only to catch something completely unexpected. Sharing here the first big fish I've caught.

4. Learning different fishing techniques

One thing I love about fishing is that there's always something new to learn. There are different types of fishing rods, baits, hooks, and techniques that you can experiment with depending on where you're fishing and what kind of fish you're trying to catch. Some techniques require patience, while others require skill and practice. I also enjoy learning how to use different equipment and understanding how each one works. For me, fishing isn't just about catching fish, it's also about improving my skills, discovering new techniques, and becoming a better angler with every fishing trip.

5. I love being alone, surrounded by trees, water and fresh air

I love fishing because it gives me the chance to be alone and be surrounded by nature. Being near the trees, water, and fresh air gives me a sense of peace and freedom. I enjoy the quiet surroundings because they allow me to relax, clear my mind, and take a break from the noise of everyday life. Sometimes, simply enjoying the scenery is already enough to make the fishing trip worth it.
